What I do

Threat intelligence

Network and SIGINT analysis, MITRE ATT&CK-based TTP mapping,1 and packet and flow analysis to identify adversary cyber activity for IC cyber missions.

1 ATT&CK is a public catalogue of adversary tactics and techniques. Mapping activity to it keeps reporting comparable from one team to the next.

Data engineering

Pipelines that don't fall over: async Python ETL from Kafka topics into Oracle, Postgres, and Neo4j — including migrating legacy stacks to Python and Kubernetes architectures.

AI-assisted security tooling

LLM-driven workflows that cut collection-to-report time by more than half, plus endpoint tooling: custom Velociraptor artifacts2 and ETL across five or more industry security scanners, Rapid7 and Nmap among them.

2 Velociraptor is an open-source endpoint collection and forensics tool. An artifact is a packaged query written in its own language, VQL.
